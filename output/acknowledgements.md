# Suggested acknowledgements

One draft per request. Requests marked *needs human review* should be checked by a person before anything is sent.

## REQ-001 - Access & Permissions - needs human review - source: template

```text
Dear colleague,

Thank you for your request. We have received it and logged it under reference REQ-001 (submitted 1 October 2026 via Service Portal).

It has been logged as Access & Permissions with Medium priority, and we aim to respond within 3 working days.

Access requests are checked against our data protection and information security policies before access is granted. We may contact you to confirm the business need and the approving manager.

Please quote REQ-001 in any further correspondence.

Kind regards,
Digital Services Team
```
