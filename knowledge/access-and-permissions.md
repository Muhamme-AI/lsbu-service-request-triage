# Access and permissions

Access requests cover granting, changing or removing access to dashboards, document libraries, SharePoint sites and other systems.

Check that the requester has given the system name, the person or role who needs access, and the level of access (read or edit). If any of those are missing, ask for them before doing the work.

External access, including external collaborators, guests, partner staff and suppliers, needs an information-governance check before approval. Confirm the business need and the approving manager. Do not grant external access from the acknowledgement alone.

Password resets must not be handled by sharing a password in email or Teams. Point the person to the self-service reset route.

A high priority access request should be assigned an owner the same day.
