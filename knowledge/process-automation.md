# Process automation

Automation requests cover reminders, form-to-list transfers, approvals and scheduled jobs.

Before building anything, confirm three facts with the requester: the trigger, the steps to automate, and who should be notified. If the request is still New and older than five days, it is overdue and should be picked up before new design work.

A high priority automation request has a response target of 1 working day. Medium priority is 3 working days. Low priority is 5 working days.

Do not turn on a flow that sends mail to people outside the organisation until the recipient list has been checked.
